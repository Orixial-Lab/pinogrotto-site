Questions people ask first

What is PinoGrotto?

PinoGrotto is an allocation and export merchant for South African estate wines. We secure wines at release, directly from estates, and deliver them with full provenance to yachts, private aircraft, residences, hotels and cruise lines internationally.

Why don't you name your estates?

Our estates prefer privacy, so we show each one by number and by the soil its vines grow in. Every estate is a registered South African producer, and your advisors can confirm identities privately, under confidentiality, before you buy.

How far ahead should a yacht order?

Everything ships from South Africa, so plan by port. Allow about three weeks before each arrival. The Voyage Planner shows the exact date to confirm each list.

Where do you deliver?

To ports, private aviation terminals, residences, hotels and cruise lines internationally, shipped from the Cape. Duty and customs for each destination are confirmed with you before shipping.

How do I know a bottle is genuine?

Every case travels under a numbered tamper-evident seal with its own temperature logger, and carries a tag that opens its passport: the estate's ground, packing, landing, release and handover, with the temperature range it saw on the way.

How do I start?

Request an introduction or email terrilyn@pinogrotto.com. Your Allocation Steward replies personally within one working day.
